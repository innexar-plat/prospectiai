# Business Rules

## i18n

- Market determined by domain
- BR: precisionia.com.br → pt bundle
- US: precisionai.innexar.app → en bundle
- SEO generator reads VITE_MARKET env var (BR or US)

## SEO

- AI crawlers allowed: GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot
- robots.txt and Cloudflare AI Audit must stay in sync
